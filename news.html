Our Fort Lauderdale Office Moves To a New Location

It’s with great pride that we’re announcing the relocation of the Fort Lauderdale office to 2765 W. Cypress Creek Rd, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33309. The office is 7,500 square feet and has over 40 employees.

Acquisition of ExactTransport.com

Indo-Pol Transport LLC Services is always looking for better ways to serve our customers. Hence, we acquired ExactTransport.com, a division that will help us serve our car shipping clientele faster and more efficiently.

Kentucky Accounting Office Moves To a New Address

Formerly at 2252 Burlington Pike Ste, 200 Burlington, KY 41005, our Kentucky accounting office is now located at 7920 Dream St, Florence, KY 41042.

Acquisition of WideLoadShipping.com

We’re thrilled to announce that we acquired Wide Load Shipping, a platform to help us safely serve customers transporting oversized and overweight loads across the country, Canada, and Mexico.
